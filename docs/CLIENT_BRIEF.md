## What Does Prela Do?

Prela Automobile AG offers the following core services:

### 1. 🚗 Autoankauf (Car Purchasing)
They buy cars of **ALL brands** and in **ANY condition** – including accident-damaged vehicles, high-mileage vehicles, and non-running cars. This is their primary business and should be **heavily emphasized** on the website.

### 2. 💰 Kostenlose Autobewertung (Free Car Valuation)
They provide a **free vehicle valuation within 24 hours**. This is the main conversion action on the website – the valuation form should appear on every page.

### 3. 🏪 Autoverkauf (Car Selling)
They also sell quality used cars to customers.

### 4. 🌍 Autoexport (Car Export)
International car export with full customs and logistics handling, including EUR.1 documents, VAT refund assistance, and transportation coordination.

### 5. 💳 Finanzierung (Financing)
Vehicle financing through Swiss finance partners.

### 6. 🔄 Inzahlungnahme (Trade-In)
They accept trade-ins with fair market valuation.

### 7. 🔧 KFZ-Werkstatt (Auto Workshop)
Their origin is as a **professional auto workshop** – this is a key differentiator. Their workshop background means they know the true value of every vehicle and can pass this knowledge advantage to customers.

## Why Was This Website Built?

### The Migration Story

The website was originally built using **Django** (Python web framework) and deployed on various hosting providers (Vercel, Render). The Django version had deployment challenges and performance issues.

The decision was made to **migrate to Astro** (a modern static site generator) because:
1. **Performance** – Static sites load faster, which is critical for SEO and user experience
2. **Simplicity** – No backend server needed; just static HTML/CSS/JS
3. **Hosting Flexibility** – Can deploy anywhere (Netlify, Cloudflare Pages, any static host)
4. **SEO** – Better Core Web Vitals scores with static output
5. **Maintenance** – No server maintenance, no database to manage

### What the Website Should Achieve

1. **Lead Generation** – The #1 goal is to get users to fill out the valuation form (Bewertung)
2. **Trust Building** – Show experience, reviews, and professionalism to convert visitors
3. **Local SEO** – Rank for "Auto verkaufen Schweiz", "Autoankauf Luzern", and related terms
4. **Brand Authority** – Establish Prela as a trustworthy, experienced Swiss auto dealer
5. **WhatsApp Contact** – Provide an easy way for visitors to reach out via WhatsApp
